<h2 align="center">
What is WLR?
</h2>
<p align="center">
WLR is a free and open-source Hypixel Duels bot, a heavily modified fork of duck dueller mainly designed for sumo
</p>
<h2 align="center">
   What gamemodes are supported?
</h2>

- ✅ Sumo
- ✅ Classic
- ✅ Boxing
- 🚧 OP

- ✅ Replay Clearing

## Boosting modes!
- ✅ Skywars
- ✅ Blitz
- ✅ Megawalls - Solos
- ✅ OP - Solos
- ✅ Sumo (With non-toggleable lobby movement)
- ✅ No Debuff
- ✅ Combo
- ✅ TNT Spleef
- ✅ Classic
- ✅ Boxing
- ✅ Bow
- ✅ UHC - Solos

*Note: these are the modes the bot will definitely support. There might be more!*

<h2 align="center">
   What features does it have?
</h2>

- ✅ Smart Aim
- ✅ Smart Strafe (Customizable)
- ✅ W-Tap
- ✅ Pre-Game Movement (Sumo only for now, sorry!)
- ✅ Bow & rod usage
- ✅ Many failsafes
- ✅ Discord Webhook Integration
- ✅ Highly Customizable :D
- ✅ Hidden from modlist sent to Hypixel
- ✅ Boosting modes
- ✅ Session HUD
- ✅ Replay Clearing
